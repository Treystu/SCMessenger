# Census of every open PR, 2026-11-08 - 42 rows, paginated

Status: filed, evidence only. No merge, refresh, label, close, review request or comment on any car was
performed to produce it. #489 was read and nothing else.

Purpose: two jobs the register asked for and did not have. First, the plan's next-action 1 is annotated
PARTIAL because its census was never completed - this completes it over every open PR, not a sample. Second,
register row A23 states its own verification method ("read `behind_by` on the exact head that would land"),
so this read is the one that settles it.

## Method

- Open set: one GraphQL pagination over `pullRequests(states: OPEN, first: 50)` until `hasNextPage` is false.
  `totalCount` 42, fetched 42, so no page cap was reached and no default page size was trusted.
- Per PR, from the same query: `isDraft`, `mergeable`, `headRefOid`, `baseRefName`, review `totalCount`, and
  `statusCheckRollup.contexts(first: 100)` with each `CheckRun.conclusion` and `StatusContext.state`
  normalized to a single casing. The first pass compared a lowercase conclusion against an uppercase state
  and reported a false "partial" for 28 heads; this pass corrects it.
- `compare/main...<head>` for every non-draft head, for `behind_by` and `ahead_by`.
- Every failing or cancelled check-run on 16 heads classified individually from its own annotation:
  B = the account-lock billing string, O = any other message. A head whose failures were not read is
  labelled `unclassified` rather than assumed.

## Findings

1. 42 open, 4 draft, 38 ready. Of the 38 ready, 28 read 5/5 required all-green; excluding the
   owner-protected #489, the landing-eligible population is 27 cars.
2. Exactly one PR in the repository carries a `ci/circleci:*` context: #653, with 12. Measured across all 42,
   so the ordering contradiction is a property of the repository and not of a sampled subset.
3. A23 is FALSIFIED IN PART. It claims "every car is BEHIND main under strict=true and therefore needs an
   update-branch". 8 of the 27 landing-eligible cars sit at `behind_by=0` - #506, #508, #521, #611, #660, #662, #663, #664. They need no refresh and no
   fresh required run; the stated 2-3 sequential-run cost applies only to the other 19.
4. The remaining red is not all billing. Eleven open PRs carry genuine (non-billing) failures, and four of
   them fail a required context: #528 (`Rust Linting`, `Lint`, `Repository Hygiene Checks`,
   `Test (ubuntu-latest)`), #538 (`Lint`, `Test (ubuntu-latest)`), #633 (`Lint`), #647
   (`Repository Hygiene Checks`). #631 fails four platform jobs, and #178/#208/#220 fail eleven between them.
   None of these appeared in the car dispositions, because the car dispositions only ever listed cars.
5. Only two eligible cars conflict: #609 and #643.
6. #662, #663 and #664 are billing-only (8, 8 and 12 annotations, all the account-lock string): new cars with
   clean content and no readable defect.
